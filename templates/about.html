About OilSense AI

OilSense AI Based Crude Oil Refinery Equipment Monitoring System


OilSense AI is an Artificial Intelligence based monitoring system developed for crude oil refinery equipment. The system uses Machine Learning to analyze live SCADA sensor values such as Pressure, Temperature, Flow Rate, Pump Speed, Energy Consumption and Alarm Status. Based on these sensor values, the AI predicts whether the refinery equipment is operating normally or developing a fault. It also estimates equipment health, confidence level, risk category and maintenance recommendations. The objective of the project is to reduce equipment failure, improve refinery safety and assist maintenance engineers through intelligent prediction.

Project Objective

The primary objective of OilSense AI is to improve refinery safety, reduce equipment downtime, minimize maintenance cost, and assist engineers in making faster maintenance decisions using Artificial Intelligence. The project demonstrates how Machine Learning can be integrated with industrial SCADA systems to perform predictive maintenance and intelligent monitoring.
